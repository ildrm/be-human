# ADR 0002: No global life score

Status: accepted and non-negotiable, 2026-09-18.

The product represents separate Life Fit dimensions with supported, strained, or unknown states plus confidence and explanation. Hard safety constraints cannot be offset by unrelated positive values. Religion, relationship style, purpose, ambition, family structure, and similar normative subjects remain preference- and impact-based. This prevents false precision, shame, and culturally imposed ranking.
